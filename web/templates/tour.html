Hey there, welcome to Kleeppr!
Let's get you started!
  • Install Kleeppr Bookmarklet

    Kleeppr Bookmarklet provides a shortcut to fill in your KleepprID in newsletter's subscription form. We strongly recommend you install it as its most convenient and effective way of using Kleeppr. Just follow above link for installation steps. Alternatively you can copy/note down your KleepprID.

  • Explore & Subscribe interesting newsletters

    Head over to Explore tab and check out hundreds of newsletters out there. Feel free to give them a try. Hit subscribe and we will redirect you to newsletter's website. Once you are there, click on bookmarklet or copy/paste your KleepprID to subscribe.

  • Come back and check Pending confirmations

    Many newsletters ask you to opt-in to the subscription before they start sending you emails. So once you subscribe, come back and check Pending Confirmations tab. Do not forget to confirm your subscriptions.

  • Check your Feed

    Each newsletter has different frequency. Depending on what and when you have subscribed, you will have to wait a bit before you start receiving issues in your feed for the first time.

    Your feed has two flavors - Recent & Snapshot. Snapshot is chronologically ordered feed of issues as they are received. Recent shows only a latest issue of each newsletter. It brings focus on all type of newsletters irrespective of their schedule.

  • Periodically review your Subscriptions

    Review all your subscriptions at one place. Easily unsubscribe or check issues of specific newsletter.

FAQ is here

  • I subscribed to a newsletter but don't see anything in feed?

    Make sure to check Pending Confirmation tab for any confirmation email.Many newsletters need user to confirm subscription first. Each newsletter has its frequency, daily or on certain day of a week etc. Please wait. If you still do not see your newsletter issue reach out to us.

  • Can I subscribe to a newsletter that is not in your list?

    Yes absolutely! This is how our list grows too.

  • What is your bookmarklet?

    Its a very simple javascript code that fills in your Kleeppr ID in email field of subscription form. It's one line javascript code, does not depend on any external script, does not collect any data.
